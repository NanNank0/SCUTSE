## Exercise 4.9

a. Show the result of inserting $3,1,4,6,9,2,5,7$ into an initially empty binary search tree.

b. Show the result of deleting the root.

### Solution A of 4.9

* BST的插法是比当前根节点大的往右放，小的往左放

### Solution B of 4.9

* 上课讲的应该是使用右子树里面的最小节点来替代，所以我们观察右子树，发现在 $\{4, 6, 9, 5, 7\}$ 里面最小的节点是 $4$
* 然鹅 $4$ 节点没有左节点，所以直接把它的右节点扯下来就行了

```mermaid
graph TB
B((1))
C((4))
D((6))
E((9))
F((2))
G((5))
H((7))
C---B
B---STAY1(( ))
B---F
C---D
D---G
D---E
E---H
E---STAY3(( ))
style STAY1 fill:#f100,stroke-width:0px
style STAY3 fill:#f100,stroke-width:0px
linkStyle 1 stroke:#0ff,stroke-width:0px
linkStyle 7 stroke:#0ff,stroke-width:0px
```
